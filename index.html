Two & Four

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Two & Four does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Your vehicles, service records, notes, costs, photos, custom services and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer never receives them.

  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data from the device.
  • If you have turned on Android's own backup to your Google account, Android may include the app's data in that backup. It is stored in your Google account under Google's terms, and the developer cannot access it.
  • Photos you take or pick are copied into the app's private storage. The app does not read the rest of your photo library.

When the app goes online

The app only connects to the internet when you tap a feature that needs it. It then contacts the service below directly, sending only what that lookup needs. Nothing identifies you, beyond the network address any internet request carries.

Camera and photos

The camera opens through your phone's camera app only when you tap Take photo. Gallery photos come through Android's photo picker, which shares only the photos you choose. The app asks for no camera, location, contacts or storage permission.

Import and export

Import reads only the files you pick. Export writes a .zip file to the place you choose, such as your Downloads folder or a cloud drive. What happens to that file afterward is up to you and the storage you picked.

Children

Two & Four is a vehicle maintenance tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
